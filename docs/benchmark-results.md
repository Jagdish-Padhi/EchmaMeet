## Surprises and Unexpected Outcomes

1. **Static Predictor Magnitude Miscalibration**: While static LLMs accurately identify conceptual vulnerabilities (such as retries exacerbating delay or missing timeouts holding connections), quantitative latency estimates diverge markedly from sandbox measurements (typically by 2x-5x magnitude ratio), underscoring the necessity of real process execution.
2. **Availability vs. Consistency Bound**: In `availability-tradeoff`, where a critical write downstream dependency was down, neither the rule-based heuristics nor the model could reach PASS without violating consistency invariants, correctly yielding an honest `TRADE_OFF` report rather than a false resolution.
